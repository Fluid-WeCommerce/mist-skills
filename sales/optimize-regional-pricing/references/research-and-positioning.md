# Research and positioning

Checked 2026-07-30. Recheck current product claims and platform behavior before using them in a real recommendation.

## Comparable products

- [ParityDeals SaaS Pricing Calculator](https://www.paritydeals.com/saas-pricing-calculator) converts a USD subscription price into localized suggestions for more than 150 countries.
- [Regional Price Calculator](https://regionalpricecalculator.com/) combines purchasing power, currency exchange, and local-market inputs to recommend country prices and export them for digital platforms.
- [Localize Prices for Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/localize-prices/id6755549612?mt=12) advertises World Bank PPP inputs, App Store tiers, manual country adjustments, and country-by-country revenue projections. Its public listing does not disclose the projection formula and has insufficient ratings for a review summary.
- [Haiku Optimizer](https://www.getoptimizer.com/) combines PPP, currency, and other heuristics with map editing, rollback, and reporting across MRR, ARR, and churn.

These products validate demand. They also mean that “PPP calculator” alone is not a differentiated submission.

## Platform and experiment evidence

- [Stripe Adaptive Pricing](https://support.stripe.com/questions/adaptive-pricing) reports 17.8% higher international revenue on average from local-currency presentation. This supports local presentment, not purchasing-power discounts, and Stripe states that the customer pays a 2–4% conversion fee.
- [Paddle localized pricing](https://developer.paddle.com/build/products/offer-localized-pricing/) supports automatic currency conversion plus country-specific overrides based on willingness-to-pay and purchasing power.
- [RevenueCat Experiments](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/experiments-v1/experiments-overview-v1) supports price-point experiments and full subscription-lifecycle metrics. [Experiment configuration](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/tools/experiments-v1/configuring-experiments-v1) can restrict enrollment by country and to new customers.

Treat every vendor uplift figure as context, not as a conversion assumption for another company.

## Differentiated position

Own the space between a recommendation and a rollout:

> Show the price a market may support, the conversion response required to justify it, exactly where the projected money comes from, and the smallest reversible experiment that can prove it.

The strongest differentiators are:

- explicit break-even conversion rather than a hidden “optimal” price;
- price-effect versus volume-recovery decomposition;
- low, base, and high scenarios with attached evidence grades;
- negative outcomes displayed as prominently as positive ones;
- experiment-ready handoff rather than automatic publishing;
- a designer-led explanatory report that makes one causal claim per chart.

## Visual reference

Keep only the transferable discipline of reduction. Use familiar charts and literal labels rather than standalone illustrations, metaphors, poster layouts, or heavily stylized social graphics.
