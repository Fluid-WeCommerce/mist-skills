# Research patterns behind the framework

The source review covered 27 identifiable commerce experiences. Nineteen accessible live PDPs received complete module audits; eight additional examples were supplemented with official indexed page material when a complete live audit was not available.

Across the 19 complete live audits, recurring modules included:

- Reviews or ratings: 18 of 19
- Ingredients or materials: 14 of 19
- How-to or use guidance: 13 of 19
- Bundles: 12 of 19
- FAQs: 12 of 19
- Shipping detail: 12 of 19
- Guarantee or risk reversal: 11 of 19
- Subscription: 10 of 19
- Science or technical mechanism: 9 of 19
- Comparison: 7 of 19
- UGC modules: 4 of 19
- Press modules: 3 of 19

These counts describe presence, not quality or causal conversion impact. Use them to understand category expectations, never as a checklist or proof that a module should exist.

The strongest recurring pattern was not page length. It was deliberate sequencing: make the product easy to understand, make the outcome easy to imagine, make the promise credible, and make the purchase feel safe.

Frequently instructive examples in the source material included Hydrant, Graza, Everyday Dose, Carpe, Foton, AG1, Gruns, Eight Sleep, Medicube, Ninja, Tarte, and Mielle. Reinspect current live pages before making present-tense claims because PDPs change.
