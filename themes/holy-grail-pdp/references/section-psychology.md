## 1. Navigation and announcement

- **Buyer question:** Can I find what I need, and am I close to a useful reward?
- **Psychology:** Cognitive ease and goal gradient.
- **Design job:** Use one concise offer or shipping promise; simplify navigation into recognizable categories.
- **Evidence/content:** Exact threshold, deadline, eligibility, or delivery promise.
- **Failure mode:** Rotating messages, oversized logos, dense menus, or fake urgency.

## 2. Hero and purchase panel

- **Buyer question:** Is this what I clicked, what does it do, and which option is right for me?
- **Psychology:** Message match, processing fluency, anchoring, and choice architecture.
- **Design job:** Repeat the traffic-source promise and visual, show the outcome, then organize variant, quantity, purchase mode, delivery, guarantee, and CTA in one decision view.
- **Evidence/content:** Rating context, total price, savings basis, subscription cadence, cancellation, shipping, returns.
- **Failure mode:** Ad-to-page mismatch, hidden offer math, too many equal choices, or a CTA that appears before required configuration.

## 3. Trust bar

- **Buyer question:** Do credible people, and enough people, believe this works?
- **Psychology:** Social proof and authority bias.
- **Design job:** Create a short high-contrast proof interruption after the offer.
- **Evidence/content:** Verified customer count plus one relevant expert, publisher, retailer, award, or certification.
- **Failure mode:** Ten weak logos, unverifiable superlatives, or authority unrelated to the claim.

## 4. Problem and transformation

- **Buyer question:** Is this my problem, and can I picture a better state?
- **Psychology:** Problem recognition, contrast effect, and future-self simulation.
- **Design job:** Make the present frustration specific and show the product visibly causing the after-state.
- **Evidence/content:** Demonstration, before/after with conditions, or a precise scenario.
- **Failure mode:** Agitating shame, exaggerating the problem, or showing an outcome the product cannot visibly explain.

## 5. Benefit hierarchy

- **Buyer question:** What changes in my life, not merely what is inside the box?
- **Psychology:** Goal salience and affect heuristic.
- **Design job:** Give the primary outcome more visual weight than supporting benefits.
- **Evidence/content:** Feature-to-consequence links, lived outcomes, sensory or convenience detail.
- **Failure mode:** Equal cards for unequal benefits, feature inventory, or vague lifestyle copy.

## 6. Mechanism

- **Buyer question:** Why should this work, and why is it different?
- **Psychology:** Causal coherence and reason-why effect.
- **Design job:** Turn ingredients, materials, sourcing, construction, or technology into a branded explanatory graphic.
- **Evidence/content:** Named inputs, causal chain, testing, sourcing, and honest limitations.
- **Failure mode:** Science theater, unexplained jargon, or a mechanism that cannot be retold in one breath.

## 7. Ritual and use

- **Buyer question:** Can I see this fitting naturally into my day?
- **Psychology:** Mental simulation, implementation intention, and habit formation.
- **Design job:** Show hands, quantity, timing, environment, effort, cleanup, and immediate reward.
- **Evidence/content:** How-to steps, recipes, model sizing, compatibility, care, or replenishment cycle.
- **Failure mode:** Ambiguous setup, missing time/effort, or polished lifestyle imagery with no usable instruction.

## 8. Evidence

- **Buyer question:** Was this measured, or is it just marketing?
- **Psychology:** Specificity effect and uncertainty reduction.
- **Design job:** Give the strongest defensible proof point a clear stage and keep its context visible.
- **Evidence/content:** Population, method, duration, source, limitations, and disclosure.
- **Failure mode:** Floating percentages, tiny methodology, cherry-picked outcomes, or fabricated precision.

## 9. Comparison

- **Buyer question:** Why should I switch from what I already use?
- **Psychology:** Contrast effect and status-quo disruption.
- **Design job:** Compare the factors buyers genuinely weigh: outcome, effort, evidence, ingredients/materials, and total ownership value.
- **Evidence/content:** Like-for-like claims, sources, total cost, and a clear best-for row.
- **Failure mode:** Straw-man competitors, hidden time frames, or letting the home product win every row dishonestly.

## 10. Community and UGC

- **Buyer question:** Are people like me getting the outcome I want?
- **Psychology:** Self-identification, social proof, and identity signaling.
- **Design job:** Curate raw UGC and reviews by audience, context, starting point, routine, duration, and tradeoff.
- **Evidence/content:** Verification, incentives, disclosure, profile, and specific use context.
- **Failure mode:** Generic praise, suspiciously uniform content, or social proof with no resemblance to the target buyer.

## 11. Objections and guarantee

- **Buyer question:** What could go wrong after I pay?
- **Psychology:** Ambiguity aversion, loss aversion, and risk reversal.
- **Design job:** Answer fit, safety, compatibility, shipping, returns, guarantee, warranty, support, and renewal where the anxiety occurs.
- **Evidence/content:** Exact window, eligibility, process, timing, and exclusions.
- **Failure mode:** Hiding terms in FAQs, vague guarantees, or introducing fees after intent is formed.

## 12. Cross-sell and bundle

- **Buyer question:** What completes the job without making me reconsider everything?
- **Psychology:** Goal completion, value anchoring, and commitment consistency.
- **Design job:** Recommend only items that complete, replenish, protect, style, or redirect the same goal.
- **Evidence/content:** Bundle contents, transparent savings, use case, and replenishment logic.
- **Failure mode:** Random inventory, artificial thresholds, or an add-on that interrupts the core purchase.

## 13. Closing decision

- **Buyer question:** Do I still know exactly what I chose, what it costs, and why it is safe?
- **Psychology:** Decision momentum and cognitive closure.
- **Design job:** Recap the configured product, offer, strongest proof, delivery, risk reversal, and CTA without restarting the pitch.
- **Evidence/content:** Preserved selections, exact total, payment methods, delivery, support, and returns.
- **Failure mode:** Lost state, surprise fees, extra page loads, or a cart that forces a second decision.

## 14. Mobile translation

- **Buyer question:** Can I understand and complete this with one thumb?
- **Psychology:** Cognitive load and motor fluency.
- **Design job:** Recompose the page with one dominant asset, state-aware sticky CTA, protected thumb zone, consistent media ratios, and preserved state.
- **Evidence/content:** Same claims and terms as desktop, placed beside the same decisions.
- **Failure mode:** Desktop stacking, buried CTA, covered checkout, pop-up collisions, extra clicks, or missing familiar payment methods.
